Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Flower Identifier. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

2. Information We Collect

Flower Identifier collects only the information necessary to provide its image recognition service.

Depending on how you use the application, we may collect:

  • Images uploaded by users for identification
  • Basic device information (such as operating system and app version)
  • Anonymous crash reports and diagnostic information
  • Application usage statistics

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or payment information.

3. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used only to:

  • Identify flowers and plants from uploaded images
  • Return recognition results
  • Improve recognition accuracy
  • Improve application performance and stability
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Protect the security of our services

4. Image Processing

Images uploaded for recognition are processed only for the purpose of generating identification results.

Unless otherwise stated, uploaded images are not permanently stored after processing.

5. Data Storage

Some temporary data may be stored during processing in order to provide recognition services.

We do not permanently retain uploaded images unless required for debugging, legal compliance, or if users explicitly provide permission.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may only be disclosed when:

  • Required by applicable law
  • Necessary to protect our legal rights
  • Required to prevent fraud or abuse of the service

7. Children's Privacy

Flower Identifier is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.

8. Third-Party Components

Flower Identifier may use third-party open-source AI models or software libraries to perform image recognition. These components process data only as necessary to provide the requested functionality.

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
